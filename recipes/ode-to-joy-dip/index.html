Ode to Joy Dip

Jammy caramelized onion and garlic folded into a tangy dairy base. Best enjoyed with tortilla chips or crusty bread.

Ingredients

  • 1 onion, diced
  • 5 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon beef bouillon concentrate
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
  • Pinch of celery salt and cracked black pepper to taste

Method

  1. Caramelize: Cook the diced onion low and slow with the smashed garlic cloves for 30 to 45 minutes until mahogany brown and jammy.
  2. Deglaze: While the onions and garlic are still hot, deglaze the pan with the Worcestershire sauce and beef bouillon concentrate. Let the mixture cool.
  3. Mix Base: In a separate bowl, combine the mayonnaise, sour cream, lemon juice, celery salt, and black pepper.
  4. Combine: Fold the cooled jammy onion and garlic mixture into the dairy base.
  5. Chill: Refrigerate for 20 to 30 minutes before serving to meld the flavors.
