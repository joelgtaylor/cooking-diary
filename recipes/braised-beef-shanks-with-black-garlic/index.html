Braised Beef Shanks with Black Garlic

Low and slow until fork-tender, finished with an emulsified pan sauce.

Ingredients

  • 0.764 kg beef shanks
  • Beef tallow (for searing)
  • Onions, carrots, celery (mirepoix)
  • Black garlic
  • Beef broth and balsamic vinegar (for deglazing/braising)
  • Butter (for emulsifying sauce)

Method

  1. Sear: Brown the beef shanks in high-smoke-point beef tallow until a dark brown crust forms.
  2. Deglaze & Aromatics: Deglaze pot with beef broth and balsamic vinegar. Add mirepoix and black garlic.
  3. Braise: Cover and braise in a 300°F (150°C) oven for 3 to 3.5 hours (alternatively, slow cooker on low for 7 to 8 hours) until fork-tender.
  4. Finish: Reduce the braising liquid and emulsify with butter before serving.
