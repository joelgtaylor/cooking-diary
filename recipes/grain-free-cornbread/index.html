Grain-Free Cornbread

Almond and coconut flour cornbread with cheddar. Sweeten with a glycine and inulin blend if desired.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups almond flour
  • 1/4 cup coconut flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar

Method

  1. Combine wet and dry ingredients, fold in cheese, and bake until golden.
