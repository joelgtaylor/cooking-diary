High-Fat Cultured Kefir Ranch

Yield: Roughly 1.5 cupsPrep: 10 minutes (+ 30 minutes to chill)

Kewpie mayonnaise meets strained kefir for a dense, tangy, umami-rich ranch.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Kewpie Mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup Strained Whole-Milk Kefir (cultured from 4.3% milk, strained to a labneh-like thickness)
  • 1/4 to 1/2 cup Liquid Kefir Buttermilk (to control viscosity)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder (or 1 finely grated fresh clove)
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • 1 tbsp fresh dill, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried dill)
  • 1/2 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Method

  1. Establish the Emulsion: In a medium mixing bowl, combine the Kewpie mayonnaise and the strained whole-milk kefir. Whisk aggressively until completely smooth.
  2. Incorporate the Aromatics: Fold in the garlic, dried parsley, dill, black pepper, and kosher salt. Stir until the herbs are evenly distributed.
  3. Dial in the Viscosity: Slowly whisk in the liquid kefir buttermilk, roughly one ounce at a time. For a dense dip, stop at roughly 1/4 cup. For a pourable dressing, continue up to 1/2 cup or more.
  4. Rest and Hydrate: Transfer to an airtight jar and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
