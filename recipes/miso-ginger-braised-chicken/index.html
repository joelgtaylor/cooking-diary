Miso-Ginger Braised Chicken Thighs

November 8, 2026Instant Pot6–7 servings50–65 minutes total (approx.)

A family-approved soy, honey and ginger braise, finished with miso and sesame oil. Rich and savoury over jasmine rice, with cool cucumber, green onions and Kewpie mayonnaise. Based on the successful 1.38 kg batch.

Ingredients

Chicken and braising liquid

  • 1.38 kg boneless chicken thighs (skinless if preferred)
  • Salt, lightly applied to the chicken before searing
  • 1–2 tbsp neutral oil for searing
  • 1½ cups chicken broth
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp freshly grated ginger
  • 5–6 cloves garlic, minced

Finish and serve

  • 3 tbsp white or yellow miso paste
  • 1½ tsp toasted sesame oil
  • Optional: 1½ tbsp cornstarch blended with 2 tbsp cold water, if gravy needs thickening
  • Jasmine rice
  • Sliced cucumber and green onions
  • Kewpie mayonnaise, drizzled over the bowls

Preferred version: No Lao Gan Ma chili crisp. It was tried on the first night and was fine, but distracted from the other flavours. Salt lightly at the start because soy sauce and miso contribute substantial salt.

Method

  1. Salt and brown: Lightly salt the boneless thighs. Heat the Instant Pot on Sauté (High), add oil, and brown in 2–3 batches, about 2 minutes per side. Remove; they do not need to be fully cooked yet.
  2. Aromatics: Briefly cook garlic and ginger, about 30 seconds. Add broth, soy sauce, honey and rice vinegar. Scrape the pot bottom thoroughly to release browned bits and avoid a burn warning.
  3. Pressure cook: Return the chicken with juices. Pressure Cook on High for 6 minutes. Allow 10 minutes natural pressure release, then vent remaining pressure. Check the thickest pieces reach at least 74°C (165°F). If not, continue cooking until they do.
  4. Finish the gravy: Remove thighs. Dissolve miso in a small bowl with a little warm braising liquid before adding it back to the pot. Gently simmer to concentrate as desired; thicken gradually with cornstarch slurry if needed. Stir in toasted sesame oil. Taste before adding extra salt.
  5. Serve: Return chicken to coat with sauce. Spoon over jasmine rice and finish with sliced cucumber, green onions and Kewpie mayonnaise.

Notes from the first batch

  • The 1.38 kg quantity worked with the same 6-minute pressure cook as the smaller planned batch; the pot takes longer to pressurize with more food.
  • The only modification to the planned recipe was salting the thighs before searing — worth repeating.
  • Green onions, cucumber and Kewpie mayo were excellent additions. Their fresh, crunchy and creamy contrast balanced the rich chicken sauce.
  • Chili crisp was not bad, but brought a competing flavour. Leave it off next time.
  • No photo was taken or provided for this batch.
Family verdict: “Another great dish!” A hit with the family. Repeat as written with pre-sear salt and the green onion, cucumber and Kewpie toppings, omitting chili crisp.
