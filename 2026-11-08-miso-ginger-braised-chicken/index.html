Miso-Ginger Braised Chicken Thighs

Nov 8, 2026 · family dinner · Instant Pot · 1.38 kg chicken thighs · another hit

After the success of our French onion beef braise, we tried a much faster pressure-braised chicken with Japanese-inspired flavours: miso, ginger, garlic, soy, honey and sesame. We served it over rice with green onions, cucumber, Kewpie mayonnaise and Lao Gan Ma chili crisp. Another great dish and a hit with the family.

Repeatable recipe: Miso-Ginger Braised Chicken Thighs in the Recipe Box.

The batch

  • 1.38 kg boneless chicken thighs, lightly salted before searing.
  • Braising liquid: 1½ cups chicken broth, 4 tbsp soy sauce, 3 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp + 1 tsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp + 1 tsp grated ginger, and 5–6 cloves garlic.
  • Finish: 3 tbsp white or yellow miso stirred into the hot cooking liquid after pressure cooking, plus 1½ tsp toasted sesame oil. Cornstarch slurry optional if the sauce needs thickening.
  • Serve over jasmine rice with sliced cucumber, green onions and Kewpie mayo. Chili crisp was also tried on the first run.

Method notes

  • The only change to the planned recipe was salting the thighs before searing. Keep that in the standard method for next time, with a light hand because the sauce contains soy sauce and miso.
  • Browned in batches with the Instant Pot Sauté function, then briefly cooked the garlic and ginger. Added broth, soy, honey and vinegar, scraped the pot bottom, and returned the chicken.
  • Pressure Cook on High for 6 minutes, followed by 10 minutes natural release before venting. The larger quantity did not need extra pressure-cooking minutes; check chicken reaches 74°C (165°F).
  • Removed the cooked chicken, dissolved miso in a little warm braising liquid, then mixed it back into the gravy. Finished with sesame oil, adjusted the sauce as needed, and returned the thighs to coat.
  • Green onions, Kewpie and cucumber were particularly successful garnishes. They provided freshness, creaminess and crunch without distracting from the miso-ginger sauce.

What to change next time

  • Leave out Lao Gan Ma chili crisp. It wasn't bad, but the strong flavour competed with the rest of the dish instead of improving it.
  • Keep the light pre-sear salting and the successful green onion, cucumber and Kewpie toppings.
  • Keep the miso as a finishing ingredient, rather than pressure cooking it with the chicken.
