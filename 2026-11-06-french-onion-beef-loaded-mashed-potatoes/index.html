French Onion Beef & Loaded Mashed Potatoes

Nov 6, 2026 · Instant Pot · two discounted beef cuts · one small onion · family favourite

A surprise standout. We had two different beef cuts to use up: 873 g of stewing beef and 334 g of inside round sandwich steak. We decided on French onion-style braised beef over mashed potatoes, using the Instant Pot to cut down the cooking time. Even with only one small onion available, the beef developed deep savoury flavour and the gravy was rich and glossy.

Full repeatable recipe: Taylor Family French Onion Beef & Loaded Mashed Potatoes in the Recipe Box.

What we actually changed

  • Used one small onion instead of the three large onions originally planned. Caramelized it well to get the most flavour out of it.
  • Omitted the bay leaf.
  • Added one cup whole milk to the finished gravy to provide enough sauce for the family. The sauce stayed brown and savoury rather than becoming particularly creamy, but was exceptionally rich.
  • Our daughter made the mashed potatoes entirely to taste, seasoning with 2% milk, sour cream, salt, pepper, garlic powder, ranch dressing and Italian seasoning. No measurements were recorded for her additions.

Method and observations

  • After browning the stewing beef, we pressure cooked it in the onion, garlic, beef broth, Worcestershire, Dijon and thyme braising liquid for 40 minutes on High, followed by 15 minutes natural pressure release.
  • The stewing beef had perfect tenderness and had absorbed a tremendous amount of flavour from the broth.
  • The thin sandwich steak was quickly seared separately and folded into the finished sauce. It was good, but clearly the weaker component; it couldn't match the flavour of the pressure-braised stewing beef.
  • The resulting gravy was dark, rich and glossy, not especially creamy despite the added milk.
  • The Instant Pot turned what normally feels like a plan-a-day-ahead braise into something manageable for a slightly late dinner.

Next time

  • Consider using only stewing beef — approximately 1.2–1.5 kg — since it was the standout cut.
  • Keep the one-small-onion adaptation, no bay leaf, and extra whole milk for the gravy.
  • Let the mashed potatoes remain an improvisation, with our daughter adjusting the ranch, sour cream and seasonings to taste.
Family verdict: “Turned out fantastic.” Excellent flavour profile, perfectly tender stewing beef, rich gravy, and delicious daughter-made mashed potatoes. Definitely worth making again.
