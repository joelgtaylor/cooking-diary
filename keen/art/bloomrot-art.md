# Bloomrot — fight preview

The approved mold-monster character sheet is integrated in `bloomrot.js`. The fight trial is an isolated preview arena; normal Bakery 2 maps, culture progression, oven rules and other worlds are unchanged. The image loads on demand when the preview starts.

The trial has10 HP, a42-tick direction-locked dash warning, a44-tick dash at8.2 pixels/tick, and75 recovery ticks. Incoming shots within115 pixels and the body lane can trigger an upward dodge. Dead, departing and distant shots cannot. A110-tick cooldown and one dodge per attack cycle prevent sustained fire from starving attacks. Shooting works during jumps and recovery, with24 hit-invulnerability ticks. Pogo hits rebound at the existing -11 velocity. Dash contact damages at most once per charge. No movement constants or soundtrack changes.

The checkpoint stands outside the sealing entry gate. Retry resets the boss and gates, preserves collected cultures/relay knowledge, and restores at least45 ammo. Defeat opens both gates and awards1000 points once per level attempt. Restart re-enters the isolated trial; Full Bakery2 clears its config and initializes the normal level. Normal-game UI has no prototype buttons.

Artwork: `bloomrot-poses.png`,1254×1254 RGBA, original generated bytes preserved. Rendered top-row crops are idle[55,50,590,544] and crouch/dash[646,150,600,450]. The idle key pose follows the actual dodge arc in this trial. Dedicated airborne/firing/defeat animation, the bespoke arena treatment and level integration remain subsequent passes. The concept image containing baked actors/projectiles is not used as a background.

## Exact character prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: character concept and key-pose sprite sheet for Crust Quest, a richly detailed late-1990s DOS pixel-art sourdough platformer.
Reference image 1: pixel craftsmanship, dark indigo outlines, expressive dough characters, organic fungal materials. Do NOT copy the friendly keeper, hats, apron, or mushroom platforms. Reference image 2: cool bioluminescent cavern palette, not an edit target.
Create THE BLOOMROT: a fast aggressive mini-boss formed from a ruined sourdough starter. A lean, springy hunched creature of torn pale golden sourdough engulfed in shaggy blue-green mold, powdery ivory fungal tufts and dark purple rootlike tendrils. Long powerful forearms, digitigrade dough legs built for rapid lunges, two sharp warm amber eyes in a shadowed face, a small amber cracked starter core visible in the chest. Three distinctive bulbous spore vents on one shoulder, powdery gray-violet fungal plates on the back. Menacing and mischievous, not cute, not a giant heavy tank; no gore. Its identity is MOLD, with fuzzy filament texture and spore sacs, not just an anthropomorphic mushroom.
Four full-body poses of EXACT SAME creature in a generous 2x2 sheet on genuine transparent background, isolated and separated, no labels or text. All face three-quarter LEFT in a side-view platformer camera, no isometric. Top left: low stalking idle stance, one long hand near the ground. Top right: compact dash windup, shoulders low, head forward, rear leg coiled, tendrils drawn back; whole silhouette clear, no motion blur. Bottom left: rapid-fire spore pose, shoulder vents flared forward like organic seedpods, leaning back with mouth snarling; three small discrete glowing pale ochre spore projectiles departing left, do not obscure the creature. Bottom right: athletic dodge jump with legs tucked under the body and arms balancing, whole body airborne; no ground or shadow.
Composition: equal character size across cells, fully visible limbs and tufts, generous transparent gutters and padding. Rich square-pixel texturing, crisp dark silhouettes, opaque bodies, cool blue-cyan rim light and restrained warm amber focal points. Readable at around 80-100 gameplay pixels tall. No smooth vector, no photographic painting, no interface, no watermark.


## Rapid-spore and half-health pass

Completed attacks alternate dash → volley → dash. Volley wind-up is48 simulation ticks, locking aim to the player position at its start. Fire four10×10 spores at4.2 pixels/tick,10 ticks apart; half-health bursts use7-tick spacing. Spores are opaque, outlined and tufted so they read against the cavern independently of colour. They collide with stone and expire after145 ticks. Existing one-way shelves do not provide spore cover. All active spores clear on defeat or retry; player invulnerability protects against multiple same-frame hits.

At5/10 health, dash attacks chain exactly one additional charge, with a fresh42-tick warning and direction lock. The second warning says ANOTHER DASH. Charge speed, duration, HP, dodge rules, player movement and75-tick final recovery are preserved. Chain eligibility is decided at attack start, so damage crossing half health during a charge cannot insert an unexpected extra dash.
