# Mycelium Relay artwork direction

Generated using the built-in image generation tool, referencing Bakery 2's gameplay scene and dough gremlin materials. This atlas is integrated into the playable mission.

The friendly Sporekeeper gives the first sequence clue in conversation a short distance back from the chamber. The integrated mission replaces the solution tablet and includes backtracking, staggered caps, and two phrases.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production-minded transparent pixel-art sprite atlas for Crust Quest, a richly detailed late-1990s DOS side-view sourdough platformer.
Input images: Image 1 is a CHARACTER MATERIAL reference (golden dough gremlins, dark outlines, flour highlights); Image 2 is a PALETTE AND ENVIRONMENT reference only (real Bakery 2 fungal cavern screenshot). Do not reproduce the screenshot, UI, text or placeholder objects.
Primary request: replace the crude puzzle props with richly textured game artwork and create a friendly cavern keeper NPC who gives the player a clue after a short backtrack.
Canvas: square, exactly three equal columns and three equal rows. NINE isolated objects, one per cell, genuinely transparent background, generous transparent gutters. No visible grid, no labels, no text, no checkerboard.
ROW ONE:
Cell 1: full-body friendly older sourdough cavern keeper in relaxed idle pose, facing three-quarter RIGHT. Warm golden dough face, kindly eyes, long expressive brows, small folded blue-violet mushroom-cap hat, moss-teal flour-dusted work apron, tiny brass spore lantern held low, root walking stick. Sturdy readable silhouette, deliberately unlike hostile round gremlins. Feet and hat fully visible. Entire character opaque.
Cell 2: EXACT SAME keeper, scale and baseline, talking gesture with free hand raised toward player, mouth slightly open, lantern and staff consistent. No speech bubble.
Cell 3: dormant relay mushroom, small closed broad warm ochre bud with curled root stem, side view, no symbol.
ROW TWO:
Cell 1: interactive cyan-teal mushroom platform with a clearly carved CRESCENT rune centered on a small front medallion.
Cell 2: interactive muted violet mushroom platform with a clearly carved THREE-PRONG FORK rune centered on a front medallion.
Cell 3: interactive honey-ochre mushroom platform with a clearly carved SQUARE SPIRAL rune centered on a front medallion.
All three platforms: same overall height and width, broad nearly flat 96-pixel-style landing surface at the top, rounded stepped overhanging cap, layered textured cap flesh, pale luminous gills, short sturdy moss-root stem below. Clear dark-plum silhouette and opaque surface. Crisp glow confined to edges, no giant light halo. Rich dithered square-pixel material detail. At gameplay size they must read as detailed organic platforms, not geometric trapezoids or icons. Rune shapes readable independently of color.
ROW THREE:
Cell 1: bloomed version of the dormant mushroom, broad pale gold-green cap, dense luminous gills and thick braided root stem; silhouette about twice dormant height, stable flat landing top. No symbol.
Cell 2: tall narrow closed ROOT DOORWAY, two organic carved dark cavern-stone uprights and arched lintel entwined with dense twisted mossy roots forming a blocking vertical curtain. Pale spore nodes suggest connection to puzzle. Whole doorway visible.
Cell 3: exact same root doorway, OPEN state, roots retracted and curled against its frame, a large truly transparent central opening. Same size, position and silhouette of stone frame as closed version.
Style: exquisite game pixel art, deliberate square pixels, dark indigo outlines, cool cyan rim light, warm lantern highlights, mottled fungal textures, dense tasteful detail like the reference backgrounds. Side-view platformer camera, NO isometric perspective. All objects fully visible, separated and grounded at their individual cell baseline; no scene or floor tile. No glossy vector shapes, no smooth painted rendering, no typography, no HUD, no watermarks. Preserve actual transparency outside each sprite and inside the open doorway.

## Integration

`mycelium-relay-atlas.png` is now consumed by `mycelium.js`. Source dimensions are1254×1254 RGBA. Crop rectangles are recorded in `RELAY_ART`; generated bytes and transparency are preserved. Small rune medallions receive exact code-rendered symbols for readability. The entrance tablet has been replaced by the keeper conversation and two-phrase mission described in `mycelium-relay.md`.
