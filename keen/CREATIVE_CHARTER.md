# Crust Quest — Creative Charter

Crust Quest: Rise of the Starter is a whimsical, slightly absurd retro action-platformer set in a fantastical world built around baking, fermentation, and culinary machinery. Its characters can be bakers, adventurers, inventors, or misfits. The world takes its strange culinary logic seriously, but the game never takes itself too seriously.

## Creative boundaries

**World and story:** Maintain a strong culinary identity. Environments, inhabitants, bosses, collectibles, and objectives should belong naturally in a world of fantastical baking and fermentation. Moonlit Bakery, Fungal Caverns, and Clockwork Ovens establish the foundation.

**Mechanics:** Be flexible. Shooting, pogo, dashing, machinery, unusual creatures, and unconventional movement are welcome when enjoyable and well designed. Mechanics do not need forced culinary explanations.

**Personalities:** Be highly flexible. Characters may have different motivations, attitudes, tools, and approaches. Vance Wick does not have to become a conventional baker.

## Design principles

- Prioritize fun over thematic literalism while preserving the world's culinary identity.
- Welcome creative absurdity that fits the tone; avoid food jokes on every object or mechanic.
- Support character-specific solutions when they improve gameplay.
- Keep the core game understandable and accessible.
- Reward exploration rather than requiring busywork.
- Favor coherent, memorable experiences over accumulating features.
- Never rely on red/green distinction alone for critical information.

## Two equally legitimate heroes

Loafy embodies a playful, resourceful, puzzle-friendly approach. Vance Wick embodies a direct, action-oriented approach. Neither is inferior or secondary. They share core physics and the same world; different solutions need not become separate campaigns or movement systems.

In Fungal Caverns, Loafy completes the musical relay puzzle and Vance can shoot open the root doorway. Both activate the same progression state. Vance's solution is an intentional father-son design decision and must be preserved.

## Creative review

1. Is it fun?
2. Does it improve the quality of the game?
3. Can it belong naturally in Crust Quest's universe without compromising what makes it fun?

Record exciting ideas unsuitable for the existing worlds for future expansion rather than forcing them into the current game. Discuss future worlds with Joel and Vance before committing to their design.

## Pre-expansion preservation commitments

Polish the existing three worlds before expanding. Preserve both heroes, their art and selection UI; approved physics, enemies, bosses and soundtrack; exploration districts; recipe unlocks and progression; and the approved fullscreen/multitouch interface.

Preserve the Devourer's combat balance, service shelter, resupply, defeat animation, and released finishing oven. Preserve the corrected relay geometry and both character solutions.

Adding worlds or characters, major mechanics, boss redesigns, soundtrack replacement, wholesale geometry changes, removing exploration, weakening Vance's solution, or fundamentally changing the fullscreen controls requires explicit approval. Do not merge polish work into main without Joel's approval.
