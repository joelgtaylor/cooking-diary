Kefir Whey Sourdough

Oct 25, 2026 · kefir whey in the mix · salt folded in late · fermented perfectly

Ratio

  • 1000 g bread flour, 430 g kefir whey, 240 g kefir, 200 g starter. The 870 g scale reading at the mix was the whey, kefir, and starter combined — not 440 g of starter, as first misread.
  • Plus 1 tbsp of kefir when the dough felt stiff at the mix, and 5 g of water with the salt. The salt itself was folded in at the third round; its exact amount wasn't written down this time.

Method notes

  • Mixed the morning of Oct 25, photos from 8:42 AM. First read at the first fold: the best-handling dough in a long time, with good gluten development.
  • First round of stretch and folds 9:42 AM; second round ~10:17 AM, salt still held back.
  • Third round ~10:47 AM: salt folded in. The dough stiffened noticeably, so 5 g of water went in with it.
  • Fourth round ~11:37 AM: the salt wasn't well integrated — the dough was separating and layered in parts. Better integrated after this round, but clearly one more round was needed.
  • Fifth (extra) round ~12:17 PM to finish integrating the salt. Folds done by 12:30 PM — dough smooth, into bulk ferment, with a check at 3:00 PM.
  • 3:00 PM check: bulk ferment was right at its limit. Shaped both loaves and put them in the fridge for a short cold retard while the oven preheated (~3:35 PM), instead of pushing bulk any further.
  • Two loaves out of the oven ~6:48 PM. Banded scoring again, good colour.

Photos

Result: Delicious, and fermented perfectly. The best-handling dough in a long time lived up to its early promise: the extra fifth fold round sorted the salt integration, catching bulk right at its limit and getting the loaves into the fridge while the oven preheated kept it from tipping over. Banded scoring opened well again, with good colour.
