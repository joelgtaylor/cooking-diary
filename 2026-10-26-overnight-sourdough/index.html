Overnight Sourdough

Oct 26–27, 2026 · fridge overnight, finished on the counter · under-fermented but delicious

Ratio

  • 1000 g bread flour, about 640 g kefir, about 45 g water, 200 g starter, 20 g salt.

Method notes

  • Mixed the evening of Oct 26, around 5:45 PM. First round of stretch and folds done ~6:46 PM — the dough was looking nicely relaxed and spread out after the fold.
  • Before bed, the dough went into the fridge for the overnight cold retard.
  • Morning of Oct 27: bulk ferment had NOT finished. Shaped two loaves anyway and left them finishing ferment on the counter (photo 8:14 AM).
  • Counter-ferment check at 9:44 AM, then the first loaf went into the oven ~10:50 AM. Lid off ~11:10 AM, doneness check ~11:33 AM, first loaf out ~11:38 AM.
  • Second loaf: lid off ~11:56 AM, doneness check ~12:17 PM. Both loaves out by ~12:26 PM.
  • Second-loaf mishaps: it was accidentally started at 500°F and turned back down to 450°F about 5 minutes into the bake with the lid on. Then time got away at the end and it got about 3 extra minutes. It didn't burn, but it is noticeably darker on the bottom.
  • Crumb read: a small band of gumminess near the top edge — read as likely under-fermented, consistent with the unfinished bulk ferment. Still delicious.

Photos

Result: Delicious, with one honest mark against it. The overnight fridge didn't finish the bulk ferment, so the loaves were shaped early and finished on the counter — and the crumb shows it, with a small gummy band near the top edge. The second loaf also survived a 500°F start and three extra minutes at the end with only a darker bottom to show for it. Banded scoring opened well on both, with good colour.
