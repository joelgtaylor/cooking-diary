Garlic Parmesan Chicken and Pasta

Oct 25, 2026 · dinner · second time making it · pasta boiled separately · turned out really well

Recipe: Garlic Parmesan Chicken and Pasta (One-Pan) from Munchin with Maddie. A one-pan dinner in about 30–35 minutes, serving 4.

Recipe

  • Chicken: 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, butterflied or beaten to about ½″ thick, rubbed with 1 tbsp Italian seasoning, ½ tsp paprika, salt & pepper; seared in oil 3–5 minutes per side to 165°F, then rested.
  • Pasta: 8 oz penne, ½ jumbo yellow onion (diced), 4–6 cloves garlic, 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp oil, 2 cups chicken broth, 1 cup finely grated parmesan, ½ cup heavy cream, 2 tbsp chopped parsley.
  • As written, the pasta cooks in the broth in the pan (covered, ~10 minutes), then the parmesan, cream, and parsley go in off the heat, and the sliced chicken is tossed back through.

What we did differently

  • This was the second time making this recipe. Last time the pasta came out a little underdone, so this time it was boiled separately in a pot until al dente, instead of cooking it in the broth in the pan.

Photos

Result: Turned out really well. The separately boiled pasta fixed last time's problem — properly al dente this time, with the creamy garlic parmesan sauce coating everything. Not a lot of photos, but a keeper of a weeknight dinner.
