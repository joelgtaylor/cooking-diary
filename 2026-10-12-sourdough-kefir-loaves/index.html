Sourdough Kefir Loaves

Oct 12–13, 2026 · two loaves, deep golden-brown crust · stunners

Ratio

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 335 g kefir (in place of water)
  • 8 g water
  • 100 g rye-flour starter, at peak

Method notes

  • Fermentolyse, then salt mixed in.
  • 3 rounds of stretch-and-folds (approx. 2:54, 3:54, 4:24 PM MDT).
  • Kefir substituted for water, aiming for ~70% hydration. Dough held tighter than typical for 70% — effective hydration likely in the mid-60s (kefir is ~90% water). Added a tablespoon of water at the last fold; dough looked much nicer after.
  • Bulk ferment with check-ins through the evening (9:14 PM photo: “getting there” — gave it one more hour).
  • Shaped, cold-retarded overnight. Baked Oct 13 morning straight from the fridge in a preheated Dutch oven.

Photos

Result: Both loaves baked successfully with excellent crust color. Kefir-for-water worked — worth repeating, possibly with the extra splash of water built into the ratio next time.
