Same-Day Sourdough

Oct 20, 2026 · double batch · no overnight retard · banded scoring

Ratio

  • 1000 g bread flour, 670 g kefir, 217 g rye-flour starter, 20 g salt. The starter overshot — 17 g more than the intended 200 g — which likely sped the bulk ferment.
  • Water by feel, about 20 g total: 8 g at the mix, ½ tbsp mid-folds, and ~1 tsp with the salt. The dough started firm and shaggy — the opposite of the slack Oct 16–17 batch.

Method notes

  • Mixed ~7:40 AM MDT. First read: possibly under-hydrated this time; reassess at the salt fold.
  • Stretch-and-folds from 8:58 AM, roughly every 30 minutes. Dough very workable early on, salt still held back.
  • ~10:15 AM: dough felt quite stiff, so ½ tbsp of water worked in — very workable after.
  • Salt folded in ~11:00 AM with ~1 tsp more water; the dough stiffened, so an extra fold round ran at 11:30 AM to make sure everything was integrated.
  • Folds done by 12:30 PM — dough smooth and domed. Bulk ferment, with a check at 3:30 PM.
  • ~4:21 PM: bulk looked a touch over-fermented — lots of bubbles, a bit sticky. Shaped both loaves and put them in the fridge for a short cold retard while the oven preheated, instead of the usual overnight.
  • Loaf 1 into the Dutch oven ~5:17 PM; lid off 5:37 PM; doneness check 5:55 PM; out ~6:03 PM. New banded scoring pattern.
  • Loaf 2 in next; lid off 6:23 PM; done ~6:52 PM.

Photos

Result: Fermentation turned out to be on point. The crumb is open and even all the way through, with no dense band at the bottom — the suspected over-ferment at the end of bulk never showed up in the crumb. The short fridge retard while the oven preheated was the right save, and the new banded scoring opened beautifully on both loaves. Good oven spring and colour across the board.
