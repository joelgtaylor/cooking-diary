Dead, departing and distant shots cannot. A110-tick cooldown and one dodge per attack cycle prevent sustained fire from starving attacks. Shooting works during jumps and recovery, with24 hit-invulnerability ticks. Pogo hits rebound at the existing -11 velocity. Dash contact damages at most once per charge. No movement constants or soundtrack changes.

## Rapid-spore and half-health pass

Completed attacks alternate dash → volley → dash. Volley wind-up is48 simulation ticks, locking aim to the player position at its start. Fire four10×10 spores at4.2 pixels/tick,10 ticks apart; half-health bursts use7-tick spacing. Spores are opaque, outlined and tufted so they read against the cavern independently of colour. They collide with stone and expire after145 ticks. Existing one-way shelves do not provide spore cover. All active spores clear on defeat or retry; player invulnerability protects against multiple same-frame hits.

At5/10 health, dash attacks chain exactly one additional charge, with a fresh42-tick warning and direction lock. The second warning says ANOTHER DASH. Charge speed, duration, HP, dodge rules, player movement and75-tick final recovery are preserved. Chain eligibility is decided at attack start, so damage crossing half health during a charge cannot insert an unexpected extra dash.
