1. Choose a world

Ready — tap a theme to enable audio.

Switching worlds stops the previous theme; press Play to hear the selection. Music alone: leave the effects idle. Effects alone: stop music, then tap below. Mixed: play the new theme while trying effects. The current theme uses its original mix for reference.

2. Try the core effects

The sequence demonstrates repeated shots and pogo, a pickup and hurt feedback. It is an audio scene, independent of gameplay.

3. Review

Try one full loop, then the action sequence over the music. Does each score fit its world’s artwork? Do the caverns feel curious and spacious, and the ovens mechanical and imposing? Is the lead pleasant over time? Are shots and pogo comfortable when repeated, and is hurt feedback clear?

This is a listening audition, not a gameplay update. No game code, scores, recipes or saves are accessed. Switching tabs pauses audio and cancels the action sequence; returning requires a tap to resume.
